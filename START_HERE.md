# Start Here

This project is now set up as a working content operation, not just a planning folder.

## Mission

Build an AI-assisted public-awareness social media brand:

> Important things people are missing, explained clearly.

Starting audience:

> Working-class and lower-middle-income adults who feel trapped by work, money pressure, and confusing systems.

## Day 1 Checklist

1. Open `trackers/ideas.csv` and pick the first approved topic.
2. Open `content/first-7-scripts.md`.
3. Record Script 1 using your real voice, face optional.
4. Post it to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and Instagram Reels.
5. Add the post URLs to `trackers/posts.csv`.
6. The next day, enter the early metrics in `trackers/metrics.csv`.

## Daily Workflow

1. Pick one topic.
2. Check the source level and claim risk.
3. Record the script.
4. Create captions for each platform.
5. Post everywhere.
6. Log URLs.
7. Log metrics after 24 hours.

Target daily effort: 30 to 60 minutes.

## Weekly Workflow

Every 7 posts:

1. Review `trackers/metrics.csv`.
2. Mark each post as `keep`, `revise`, or `kill`.
3. Identify the best pillar, format, and viewer payoff.
4. Mine comments for viewer language.
5. Pick the next 7 topics.
6. Rebuild the ready-to-post queue.

## Operating Rule

The goal of the first 30 days is not instant money. The goal is proof:

- What topics make people stop?
- What hooks make people watch?
- What posts get comments, shares, saves, and follows?
- What can be repeated without burning out?

## Folder Map

- `docs/`: strategy, analysis, roadmap, and rules.
- `trackers/`: CSV files you can open in Google Sheets, LibreOffice, Excel, or import into Notion/Airtable.
- `content/`: scripts, checklists, and ready-to-record material.
