# Pre-Post Checklist

Use this before publishing every video.

## Script Check

- The first sentence is clear.
- The viewer payoff is obvious: seen, warned, informed, mistake avoided, or next step.
- The script sounds like a real person, not a lecture.
- The main claim is easy to understand.
- The ending does not beg for engagement.

## Accuracy Check

- Source level is marked.
- Claim risk is marked.
- High-risk claims have a source.
- Opinion is not presented as fact.
- The script does not give individualized legal, medical, or financial advice.

## Platform Safety Check

- No copied video is being reposted as the main value.
- Third-party content is transformed with original commentary.
- AI-generated realistic media is avoided unless disclosure is handled.
- No employer, patient, private person, or sensitive situation is identifiable.
- Sponsorship or affiliate disclosures are included if needed.

## Production Check

- Captions are readable.
- Audio is clear enough.
- Video length matches the intended platform.
- Caption variants are ready.
- Post URL will be logged after publishing.
