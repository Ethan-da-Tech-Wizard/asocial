# Conflict Media Moderation Rules

Use these rules for any Israel/Palestine-related Page or post.

## Pin This On Heated Posts

Discuss the framing, source, or claim. Do not attack civilians, religions, ethnic groups, or other commenters.

## Hide Or Delete

- Slurs.
- Dehumanizing language.
- Celebration of death, injury, displacement, or fear.
- Calls for violence.
- Support or praise for violent organizations.
- Doxing or private personal information.
- Harassment between commenters.
- Graphic content posted for shock.
- Unsourced claims repeated as certainty after correction.

## Reply When Useful

- "Can you share the source you are using?"
- "Criticize the policy or claim, not a whole people."
- "That claim needs verification before we repeat it as fact."
- "This Page does not allow celebration of civilian harm."
- "If you see missing context, add a source."

## Do Not Argue Forever

One correction is enough. After that, hide, delete, restrict, or move on.

## Daily Review

Every day, record:

- Number of comments hidden or deleted.
- Repeated phrases people used.
- Useful questions people asked.
- Claims that need sources.
- Whether the discussion stayed worth hosting.
