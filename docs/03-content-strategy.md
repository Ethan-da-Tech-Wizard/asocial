# 03 Content Strategy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

## Brand Position

The brand should be built around public-awareness content:

> Important things people are missing, explained clearly and quickly.

The tone should be direct, human, useful, and grounded. It should not feel like a corporate news page or a drama account. The viewer should feel like someone is cutting through noise and helping them notice something that matters.

## Core Promise

Follow this account if you want important things brought to your attention before they pass you by.

## Recommended Starting Formats

### Format A: "You Need To Know This"

Structure:

1. Hook: Say the overlooked thing.
2. Context: Explain why it matters.
3. Impact: Explain who it affects.
4. Takeaway: Tell the viewer what to remember.

### Format B: "The Hidden Reason"

Structure:

1. Hook: "The hidden reason X happens is..."
2. Explanation.
3. Example.
4. Viewer question.

### Format C: "I Used To Think..."

Structure:

1. Old belief.
2. New realization.
3. Evidence or lived experience.
4. Lesson.

### Format D: "Three Things"

Structure:

1. "Three things people are missing about X."
2. Point 1.
3. Point 2.
4. Point 3.
5. Short close.

### Format E: "Receipts And Context"

Structure:

1. Show or reference the source.
2. Explain the plain meaning.
3. Explain why people should care.
4. Avoid overclaiming.

## Posting Cadence

The first goal is data, not perfection.

Recommended minimum:

- 1 short-form video per day for 30 days.
- 3 to 5 X posts per week if X is used.
- 1 weekly review of metrics and lessons.

Recommended stretch:

- 2 short-form videos per day for 30 days.
- 1 longer YouTube video every 1 to 2 weeks once winning topics appear.

## Content Rules

- Make original commentary the main value.
- Do not simply repost clips.
- Do not rely on fake urgency.
- Verify claims before posting.
- Keep a source list for factual videos.
- Favor clear speech over over-edited chaos.
- Repeat formats that work.
