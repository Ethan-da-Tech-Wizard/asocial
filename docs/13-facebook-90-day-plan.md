# 13 Facebook 90-Day Plan

Last updated: September 14, 2026

## Decision

Start with one Facebook creator presence, not two competing Facebook brands.

Recommended setup:

> One Facebook Page for the public-awareness brand, managed from the creator's personal account.

Use the personal profile only to support the Page selectively, such as sharing the best posts or inviting interested people. Do not split original daily content across two separate Facebook creator accounts during the first 90 days.

## Why One Facebook Presence

One account gives the project cleaner data, less burnout, and a stronger chance of repeatable signal.

Two accounts sound like more opportunity, but they create immediate problems:

- Twice the posting decisions.
- Split metrics.
- Split comments.
- Split identity.
- More moderation.
- More risk of inconsistent quality.
- Harder weekly analysis.

The first 90 days should answer one question:

> Can this Facebook presence repeatedly create meaningful reach, comments, shares, saves, follows, and eventually monetization progress?

Two accounts make that answer harder to see.

## Page vs Professional Mode

Use a Page if the brand is meant to become an asset separate from the creator's personal life.

Page advantages:

- Separate public brand identity.
- Easier to keep personal life separate.
- Page access can be delegated later.
- Can be managed through Meta business tools.
- Better fit for a future media brand.

Professional mode advantages:

- Better if the creator wants the personal profile to be the public identity.
- Existing friends and profile history can help early distribution.
- Useful for a highly personal creator brand.

For this project, the better default is a Page because the brand is semi-personal, not fully personal.

## Account Structure

Create or use one Page with:

- Brand promise in bio: Important things people are missing, explained clearly.
- Category: Digital creator, media/news company, or public figure depending on available options.
- Profile image: simple face photo or readable brand mark.
- Cover image: plain brand statement or clean visual.
- Call to action: Follow.
- About section: Plain explanations about work, money, and systems for people trying to keep up.

Optional personal profile role:

- Share 1 to 2 Page posts per week.
- Invite friends who would genuinely care.
- Do not spam friends with every post.
- Keep personal posts separate unless they support the brand.

## Facebook Content Mix

Facebook should not be treated as only Reels. Meta's current Content Monetization direction includes Reels, longer videos, Stories, photos, and text posts for eligible creators, and Meta has emphasized original content, qualified views, deeper engagement, and longer watch time.

Use this weekly mix:

- 5 short Reels.
- 2 text/photo explainers.
- 2 Stories.
- 1 comment-response post.
- 1 longer video every 2 weeks after the first month, if a topic proves itself.

The Reels create discovery. Text/photo posts build trust and shares. Stories keep the Page active. Comment-response posts train the audience to participate.

## 90-Day Goals

### Days 1-30: Build The Signal

Goal:

> Prove which Facebook topics and formats create meaningful response.

Actions:

- Publish 1 original Reel per day for 30 days.
- Also publish 2 text/photo explainers per week.
- Track every post in `trackers/posts.csv`.
- Log metrics after 24 hours, 72 hours, and 7 days when possible.
- Reply to useful comments.
- Save viewer phrases for future hooks.

Primary metrics:

- Views.
- 3-second hold or early retention if available.
- Average watch time.
- Comments.
- Shares.
- Saves.
- New followers.
- Profile visits.
- Follows per 1,000 views.
- Shares per 1,000 views.

Do not judge the whole project by revenue in this phase.

### Days 31-60: Repeat The Winners

Goal:

> Turn the best Facebook signals into repeatable formats.

Actions:

- Identify the top 5 Reels and top 3 non-Reel posts.
- Choose 2 strongest content lanes.
- Batch 10 new posts in the strongest lane.
- Start 1 named series if a pattern is clear.
- Turn the best short topic into a 3 to 6 minute Facebook video.
- Check Professional Dashboard and Monetization tab weekly.

Decision rules:

- Keep a format if it beats baseline more than twice.
- Revise a format if comments are strong but retention is weak.
- Kill a format if it gets weak response after 3 attempts.

### Days 61-90: Prepare For Monetization

Goal:

> Build toward Facebook monetization eligibility while protecting trust.

Actions:

- Continue 5 Reels per week.
- Publish 1 longer video per week from proven topics.
- Publish 2 text/photo explainers per week.
- Create a simple free resource only if comments reveal repeated audience pain.
- Complete Facebook Content Monetization interest steps if available.
- Review originality and monetization policy status weekly.

By Day 90, decide:

- Best pillar.
- Best format.
- Best viewer payoff.
- Whether Facebook deserves more effort than other platforms.
- Whether a second account is justified.
- Whether to expand into longer videos regularly.

## When A Second Facebook Account Makes Sense

Do not start with a second account.

Consider a second Page only after Day 90 if all of these are true:

- The first Page has a clear winning lane.
- The creator can maintain the first Page without burnout.
- A second audience is genuinely different.
- The content would confuse the first Page if mixed together.
- There is enough content supply for both.

Examples where a second Page could make sense later:

- One Page becomes work/money systems commentary.
- Another Page becomes local alerts for a specific city or region.
- One Page becomes creator economy education.
- Another Page becomes personal build-in-public.

Until then, keep one Page and use tags in the tracker instead of creating a second brand.

## Facebook-Specific Content Rules

Do:

- Film or produce original content.
- Add real commentary, explanation, or lived experience.
- Use captions.
- Ask grounded questions.
- Reply to comments that reveal real pain or confusion.
- Keep sources for factual claims.

Avoid:

- Reuploading other people's videos.
- Stitching clips together without meaningful new value.
- Low-effort reaction content.
- Engagement bait.
- Overclaiming legal, medical, financial, or policy topics.
- Posting only Reels and ignoring text/photo posts.

## Weekly Facebook Review

Every week, answer:

1. Which post created the most followers per 1,000 views?
2. Which post created the most shares per 1,000 views?
3. Which topic created the most useful comments?
4. Which format was easiest to produce?
5. Which post should become a longer video?
6. Which comment should become the next post?
7. Is the Page closer to monetization eligibility or just busier?

## Verdict

Use one Facebook Page for 90 days.

Make it original, semi-personal, and measurable. Let the Page prove a repeatable lane before splitting attention into a second account.
