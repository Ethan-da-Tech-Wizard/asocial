# 11 AI Prompts

Last updated: September 14, 2026

## Purpose

These prompts are reusable commands for making the content system more hands-off. They are written so the creator can paste them into an AI assistant and get usable content drafts.

## Topic Generation Prompt

```text
Act as the strategist for my AI-assisted public-awareness social media brand.

Brand promise: Important things people are missing, explained clearly.
Audience: Working-class and lower-middle-income adults who are tired, financially pressured, and trying to understand the systems affecting their lives.
Pillars: work and survival, money awareness, systems explained.
Formats: "Nobody tells you this about...", "The quiet way this costs people...", "Here is the part they do not explain..."

Generate 20 short-form video topics. For each topic, include:
- pillar
- hook
- why it matters
- likely viewer emotion
- whether it needs fact-checking
- 1 sentence angle

Prioritize topics that can be made with no money, simple visuals, and original commentary.
```

## Script Draft Prompt

```text
Write a short-form video script for this topic:

Topic:
Hook:
Angle:
Audience:

Requirements:
- 45 to 75 seconds
- plain spoken language
- direct first sentence
- no fake urgency
- no made-up facts
- include a human point of view
- end with a thoughtful line or simple comment prompt

Give me:
1. final script
2. caption
3. 5 title options
4. 5 hashtag options
5. 3 alternate hooks
```

## Caption Variants Prompt

```text
Create platform-specific captions for this short-form video.

Script/topic:

Create:
- TikTok caption
- YouTube Shorts title and description
- Facebook Reels caption
- Instagram Reels caption
- optional X post

Rules:
- keep the meaning consistent
- do not overhype
- do not use too many hashtags
- make each platform version feel natural
```

## Repurpose Prompt

```text
Turn this one approved idea into multiple content assets.

Idea:
Script:

Create:
1. TikTok/Reels/Shorts caption
2. Facebook text post
3. X post
4. YouTube long-form video outline
5. follow-up short-form idea
6. comment prompt
```

## Weekly Analysis Prompt

```text
Act as my content analyst.

Here are my posts and metrics for the week:

[paste metrics]

Analyze:
- top 5 posts
- worst 5 posts
- best hooks
- best content pillars
- best formats
- strongest audience emotions
- what to stop doing
- what to repeat
- 10 topics for next week
- whether we have an early signal or breakthrough signal

Use the breakthrough definitions:
- early signal: 3 posts in the same format beat baseline
- breakthrough: repeatable format, clear pillar, meaningful comments/shares/saves, and follower growth from multiple posts
```

## Comment Mining Prompt

```text
Analyze these comments and turn them into content ideas.

Comments:

Return:
- repeated viewer pain points
- viewer language I should reuse
- misconceptions to address
- 10 follow-up video ideas
- 5 comment replies
- 3 possible long-form video topics
```

## Daily Operator Prompt

```text
Run today's content workflow.

Use my brand:
- Important things people are missing, explained clearly.
- Audience: working-class and lower-middle-income adults.
- Pillars: work and survival, money awareness, systems explained.

Give me:
1. 5 topic options for today
2. rank them by easiest, strongest, and most monetizable
3. draft the best script
4. give me caption variants
5. give me a simple recording plan
6. tell me what metric to watch after posting
```
