# 09 Decisions Made

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This document removes unnecessary decision fatigue. The creator can override these choices later, but the project should move forward with these defaults.

## Strategic Decision

Build an AI-assisted public-awareness media brand for working-class people who want important life, work, money, and system issues explained clearly.

## Brand Model

Use a semi-personal brand.

Reason:

- Fully personal can feel vulnerable and tiring.
- Fully anonymous can feel generic.
- Semi-personal allows trust, lived experience, and flexibility.

## Working Brand Promise

Important things people are missing, explained clearly.

## Starting Audience

Working-class and lower-middle-income adults who are tired, financially pressured, and trying to understand the systems affecting their lives.

## Starting Pillars

Use only these three for the first 30 days:

1. Work and survival.
2. Money awareness.
3. Systems explained.

Do not start with too many lanes.

## Starting Formats

Use these three:

1. "Nobody tells you this about..."
2. "The quiet way this costs people..."
3. "Here is the part they do not explain..."

## Platform Priority

Post every short-form video to:

1. TikTok.
2. YouTube Shorts.
3. Facebook Reels.
4. Instagram Reels.

Use X only if a topic works naturally as text or if there is extra time.

## Monetization Priority

The long-term money path is:

1. YouTube long-form.
2. Facebook content monetization.
3. TikTok Creator Rewards.
4. Sponsorships and affiliates.
5. Digital products or templates.

## AI Role

AI owns:

- Topic generation.
- Research organization.
- Hook drafting.
- Script drafting.
- Caption drafting.
- Repurposing.
- Weekly analysis.
- Backlog maintenance.

Creator owns:

- Final approval.
- Voice.
- Lived experience.
- Recording.
- Moral judgment.
- Whether something feels true enough to say.

## Starting Cadence

Post 1 original short-form video per day for 30 days.

Do not try to make 5 accounts, 5 brands, or 5 niches. One daily original video is enough to create useful data.

## Starting Production Style

Use simple, repeatable production:

- Talking voice or face.
- Auto captions.
- Screenshot or headline when useful.
- Plain background.
- Strong first sentence.
- Minimal editing.

Do not make production quality the bottleneck.

## First Breakthrough Target

The first target is not revenue. The first target is a repeatable signal:

- One content format beats baseline multiple times.
- One pillar clearly performs better than the others.
- Comments show people feel seen, warned, or informed.

## Current Recommended Next Action

Create the first 30-day content backlog:

- 30 topics.
- 30 hooks.
- 15 scripts ready.
- 7 videos recorded or ready to record.
