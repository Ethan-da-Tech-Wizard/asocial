# 06 Roadmap

Last updated: September 14, 2026

## Phase 0: Setup

Goal: Prepare the content operation without spending money.

Tasks:

- Use a semi-personal public-awareness brand.
- Use this working brand promise: "Important things people are missing, explained clearly."
- Create or clean up accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and optionally X.
- Create a simple idea database.
- Create a simple metrics tracker.
- Use the first 3 content pillars: work and survival, money awareness, systems explained.
- Have AI draft 50 hooks.
- Have AI draft 20 short scripts.
- Have the creator approve or reject scripts in batches.

Exit criteria:

- Accounts exist.
- Database exists.
- First 20 scripts are ready.
- First 7 videos are ready or outlined.

## Phase 1: 30-Day Posting Test

Goal: Generate enough posts to learn what the audience responds to.

Tasks:

- Post 1 short-form video per day for 30 days.
- Repost each video to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and Instagram Reels.
- Track views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows, and notes.
- Do one weekly review.
- Use AI to generate captions, hashtags, alternate hooks, and weekly performance summaries.

Exit criteria:

- 30 videos posted.
- At least 120 total platform posts created through repurposing.
- Top formats and topics identified.
- At least 5 posts show above-baseline engagement signals.

## Phase 2: Format Refinement

Goal: Stop guessing and double down on what works.

Tasks:

- Identify the top 5 posts.
- Identify the worst 5 posts.
- Compare hooks, topics, video length, and opening lines.
- Choose 3 repeatable formats based on data.
- Create 30 more videos using the strongest formats.
- Begin turning the top 3 short topics into longer YouTube scripts.

Exit criteria:

- 3 reliable formats selected.
- Clear evidence of which content pillars perform best.

## Phase 3: Monetization Path

Goal: Start aiming at platform-specific monetization milestones.

Tasks:

- Track TikTok Creator Rewards eligibility.
- Track YouTube Partner Program eligibility.
- Track Facebook Content Monetization eligibility or interest form status.
- Track Instagram monetization options.
- Decide whether X Premium is worth the cost later.

Exit criteria:

- Clear monetization dashboard.
- At least one platform has measurable progress toward eligibility.

## Phase 4: Long-Form Expansion

Goal: Turn proven short-form topics into stronger income assets.

Tasks:

- Pick top-performing short-form topics.
- Create 5 to 10 minute YouTube videos from the strongest ideas.
- Use Shorts and TikToks to send interest toward long-form videos.
- Build playlists around content pillars.

Exit criteria:

- First 3 long-form videos published.
- Short-form topics are feeding long-form ideas.

## Phase 5: Revenue Diversification

Goal: Avoid relying only on platform payouts.

Possible revenue streams:

- Platform ad/reward payouts.
- Sponsorships.
- Affiliate links.
- Digital guides.
- Paid community.
- Consulting or content services.
- Templates or trackers.

Exit criteria:

- At least one non-platform-payout revenue experiment tested.

## First 7 Days

Day 1:

- Create the tracker.
- Have AI draft 50 hooks across the 3 decided pillars.
- Approve the best 15 hooks.

Day 2:

- Have AI write 10 scripts.
- Approve or revise 5 scripts.
- Record 2 videos.

Day 3:

- Post first video to all platforms.
- Record 2 more.

Day 4:

- Post second video.
- Review comments and save new ideas.

Day 5:

- Post third video.
- Have AI draft 10 more scripts from comments, metrics, and saved topics.

Day 6:

- Post fourth video.
- Check retention and shares.

Day 7:

- Post fifth video.
- Do the first weekly review.

## Decision Points For The Creator

These are no longer open strategic decisions. The defaults are:

1. Brand model: semi-personal.
2. First pillars: work and survival, money awareness, systems explained.
3. Presentation: creator voice first, face optional, AI support behind the scenes.
4. Posting cadence: 1 original short-form video per day for 30 days.
5. Monetization target: short-form discovery first, YouTube long-form and Facebook for stronger monetization.

The creator can override these later, but the project should move forward with these defaults unless data proves they are wrong.
