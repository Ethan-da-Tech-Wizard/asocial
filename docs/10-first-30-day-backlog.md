# 10 First 30-Day Backlog

Last updated: September 14, 2026

## Purpose

This backlog gives the project enough starting material to avoid daily decision fatigue. The creator should not need to wake up and ask, "What do I post today?" The system should already have options.

## Selected Content Pillars

- Work and survival.
- Money awareness.
- Systems explained.

## Selected Formats

- "Nobody tells you this about..."
- "The quiet way this costs people..."
- "Here is the part they do not explain..."

## Posting Rule

Post one original short-form video per day for 30 days. Each video should be repurposed to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and Instagram Reels.

## 30-Day Topic Queue

### Day 1

Pillar: Work and survival.

Hook: Nobody tells you this about leaving work you hate.

Angle: Leaving a draining job is not just a motivational decision. It requires replacing income, identity, routine, and fear.

Script direction: Explain that wanting out is valid, but the escape plan needs a system, not just frustration.

### Day 2

Pillar: Money awareness.

Hook: The quiet way people lose their paycheck before they spend it.

Angle: Fixed bills, fees, debt payments, subscriptions, transportation, and food costs can consume income before a person feels any progress.

Script direction: Frame this as a system problem plus a tracking problem.

### Day 3

Pillar: Systems explained.

Hook: Here is the part of healthcare work people do not explain.

Angle: Healthcare workers can be praised publicly while being overloaded privately.

Script direction: Use lived experience carefully without naming employers or patients.

### Day 4

Pillar: Work and survival.

Hook: A job can pay you and still keep you stuck.

Angle: Stability can become a trap when the schedule, exhaustion, or wages prevent growth.

Script direction: Explain the difference between income and mobility.

### Day 5

Pillar: Money awareness.

Hook: This is why "just get a job" does not solve everything.

Angle: Work helps, but wages, rent, debt, childcare, transportation, and health costs can still leave people unstable.

### Day 6
